## IMX519 Short Capture 2026-09-24

The already configured Arducam IMX519 was tested over SSH on the owner's Q6A.
The board reported Ubuntu 24.04.4 LTS, kernel `7.0.11-6-qcom` on AArch64.
The `imx519` driver bound to I²C `20-001a`, the `ak7375` lens driver to
`20-000c`, and the sensor appeared in the Qualcomm CAMSS media graph through
`msm_csiphy3`. The installed libcamera identified the camera as `imx519` with
the `simple` pipeline (`v0.0.0+1-de1e9bc0-dirty`, built 2026-09-02).

```sh
cam --camera 1 --stream role=viewfinder,width=640,height=480 \
  --capture=30 --file=/tmp/imx519-long-#.ppm
```

This produced 30 640 × 480 PPM frames, sequences 0–29 without gaps
or capture errors. The reported inter-frame intervals were about 12.5 ms
(approximately 80 fps) for this 0.36-second run. Libcamera configured
640 × 480 ABGR8888 output from a 1280 × 720 RGGB RAW10 sensor mode. This is
only a short capture result, not a sustained frame-rate qualification.

Visual inspection of the first run's last frame found a very dark image with
no recognizable scene detail. After the owner pointed the camera at an
illuminated scene, a 160-frame run in the same mode produced a recognizable
color image. Its final metadata reported 12,038 µs exposure and the maximum
sensor gain value, 960. In a separate 1920 × 1080 still-stream run, all 60
frames arrived at about 30 fps; the final metadata reported 30,988 µs and
gain 960. The larger image was brighter but its highlights were clipped.

The `ak7375` lens subdevice accepted and retained manual `focus_absolute`
positions 0, 1000, 2000, 3000 and 4000 during separate 1920 × 1080 runs.
On this particular scene and distance, position 2000 gave a visibly sharper
monitor image than 0, 3000 or 4000. The lens position was restored to 0.
This confirms useful manual focus movement. The installed `cam --list-controls`
exposed only Contrast and Gamma, and the lens subdevice exposed
`focus_absolute` without an autofocus mode. A later one-off contrast-focus
sweep compared 1920 × 1080 frames at positions 1500, 1750, 2000, 2250 and
2500. An upper-center image sharpness score was 16.01, 21.86, 45.67, 32.17
and 16.87, respectively, so the sweep selected 2000 for a new photo. This
does not establish built-in or continuous autofocus on the Q6A.
Libcamera reported no `imx519.yaml` for its simple IPA and no IMX519 sensor
helper, falling back to `uncalibrated.yaml`. Exposure, color and autofocus
tuning remain unqualified. An attempted sensor color-bar check was
inconclusive: libcamera reset `test_pattern` from Color Bars to Disabled
during camera setup.

Representative PPM and PNG frames are in the local, Git-ignored
`var/q6a-imx519/` directory. The original capture files also remain in the
Q6A's `/tmp`; no image is committed to this repository.

## IMX519 Contrast AF 2026-09-25

The [IMX519 working kit](../README.md) now contains the
saved driver/overlay sources and a separate private libcamera build with
single and continuous contrast AF. The
[autofocus investigation](../research/autofocus.md)
records controls, build
provenance, physical checks and the remaining phase-data path. An 1800-frame
1080p run completed at 30.000 fps with contiguous sequence numbers and no
reported errors. A 1080p photo after one AF scan is retained under the ignored
`var/q6a-imx519/` directory. PDAF is not available: the sensor metadata pad
has no outgoing media link through CAMSS on the installed kernel.
